What changed on this corner

A $2.5 billion district is going up
three quarters of a mile west.

The Palo District, on the 77.9-acre former Fiesta Mall site, is planned around a 25,000-seat stadium for professional women's soccer, a $100 million women's health campus, 600 hotel rooms across two properties, plus retail and office.

Mesa rezoned it as a theme park district in November 2025, which lets the district issue project-backed bonds and levy its own sales tax. Construction on the stadium, the first retail and hospitality and the health campus begins this summer. The hotels open in early 2028.

A new Sprouts opens November 6 at Alma School and Southern, 0.44 miles west, inside a 15.8-acre development on the northeast corner.

We are not inside the Palo District. We are three quarters of a mile east of it on the same arterial — close enough to catch its traffic, established enough to be the practical address rather than the expensive new one. With nearly three acres of parking and a clinic already open.
